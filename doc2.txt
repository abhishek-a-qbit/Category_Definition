Authoritative domains for software category definitions
Organized by category of source, with sub-path notes where relevant. The sub-path discipline matters as much as the domain — as the ABM test showed, the same root domain can host both gold-standard analyst content and review-platform content.
Major industry analysts (broad coverage)
Gartner
blogs.gartner.com — analyst blog network, named bylines
gartner.com/en/articles — curated articles, including content from acquired firms (TOPO)
gartner.com/en/sales/glossary, gartner.com/en/marketing/glossary, gartner.com/en/information-technology/glossary, etc. — short canonical entries
Drop: gartner.com/reviews (Peer Insights), gartner.com/en/digital-markets (Capterra/GetApp/Software Advice)
Forrester
forrester.com/blogs and go.forrester.com/blogs — analyst-bylined posts
forrester.com/blogs/author/{name}/ — per-analyst hubs, the highest-value crawl target
forrester.com/analyst-bio/ — analyst credentials
Note: Forrester's free tier is unusually clean; no major review-platform subdivision to filter out
IDC
idc.com/getdoc.jsp and idc.com/research — most content gated, but free briefs and press releases occasionally surface category framings
blogs.idc.com — analyst commentary

Conference and event content (where transcripts and slides exist)
Useful for category framings that haven't been written up elsewhere; treat as fragmentary SME content.
RSA Conference and Black Hat archives — security

Salesforce Dreamforce, HubSpot INBOUND — CRM and martech
Gartner Symposium and Forrester Summit recap content

KubeCon, DevOpsDays — cloud-native and DevOps
Wikipedia — limited use
Useful for mature, settled categories with strong citation lists. Quality varies. Not SME-authored in the strict sense (consensus-edited), but the talk page and reference list often surface SME sources Cuspera should crawl directly. Treat Wikipedia as a discovery tool, not a citable source.
What's deliberately not on this list
Vendor sites of any kind. G2, TrustRadius, Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice, SourceForge listing pages, Crozdesk, FinancesOnline, GoodFirms, Clutch, and similar review aggregators. Gartner Peer Insights and Gartner Digital Markets specifically (review platforms inside the Gartner brand). SEO-driven "ultimate guide" content regardless of host. LinkedIn aggregator and content-mill posts. Medium posts unless authored by a credentialed SME with a strong track record. Reddit, Hacker News, Stack Exchange — useful for sentiment, not for definitions.
